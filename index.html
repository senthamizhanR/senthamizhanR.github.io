Research

Synchrony, swarms and collective behaviour.

My thesis gives a theoretical, computational and data-driven account of collective dynamics in Kuramoto and swarmalator models. Swarmalators are agents that synchronise in phase and aggregate in space at the same time.

Swarmalators

Second-order harmonics, phase frustration and frequency-weighted coupling, and the clustered, chimera, translational and mixed states each one produces.

Kuramoto synchronisation

Frequency-weighted Kuramoto oscillators under biharmonic coupling: synchronisation thresholds, hysteresis and the transitions between collective states.

Analytical theory

Mean-field theory, self-consistency arguments and linear stability analysis, with each predicted boundary checked against direct simulation.

Data-driven dynamics

A U-Net trained on multichannel statistical maps of swarm dynamics that segments the parameter plane and locates a state boundary theory cannot reach.
